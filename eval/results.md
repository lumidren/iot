# Evaluation results

Generated by `guardian eval` on 2026-11-20 (Windows AMD64, Python 3.12.16).
Seeds: [1, 2, 3]. Per seed: 2.0 days of learning (0.5 held out for calibration), then a 24-hour test day. Each of the 8 devices suffers each of the 8 scenarios once (5 minutes, random time of day). Alert threshold: threat >= 61 (quarantine).

> All traffic and attacks are simulated, and the simulator and the detector were written by the same person. These numbers show the pipeline works and how its parts compare. They are not real-world detection rates.
